About the Platform

Dialogue is the heart of how women lead change

Dialogue is the core mechanism through which women drive conflict analysis, counter violent extremism, and address shared community and environmental issues.

Through partnerships with local leaders and institutions, the platform solidifies women's role as key players in decision-making and conflict resolution.

Our Aims

Four aims, one shared voice

What the platform sets out to achieve with women leaders across the region.

01

Engage in Dialogue

Engage women in dialogue on shared community and environmental issues, demonstrating how their genuine presence changes the solutions proposed.

02

Equip with Skills

Equip women with practical skills in conflict analysis and dialogue facilitation, enabling them to lead conflict resolution processes in their communities.

03

Prevent Extremism

Strengthen women's ability to recognize early signs of violent extremism and contribute to community-level prevention.

04

Build a Network

Establish a documented, connected network of women leaders across the Arab region and Africa who exchange experience and support one another.

Our Approach

Dialogue as the core mechanism

Women's leadership in dialogue shapes three areas of impact in their communities.

  1. I

    Conflict Analysis

    Understanding the roots and dynamics of conflict to lead resolution processes.

  2. II

    Countering Violent Extremism

    Recognizing early warning signs and supporting community-level prevention.

  3. III

    Community & Environment

    Addressing shared community and environmental issues together.
