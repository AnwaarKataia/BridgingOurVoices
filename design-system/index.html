14 · Rules

Do & don't

Do: logo on navy → logo-dark.jpgHeader, footer and any navy block use the navy-background logo.
Do: logo on light → logo.pngTransparent version for paper / white backgrounds.
Do: gold as accentPrimary CTAs, numbers, eyebrows, card top rules.
Do: headings regular weightNewsreader 400, one colour per heading.
Don't: bold or italic displayNo highlighted words inside headings.
Don't: rounded corners2px radius only; pills are for chips.
Don't: gold text on paperUse --bov-gold-deep for readable gold text on light backgrounds.
Don't: new coloursAdd a token to tokens.css first, then log it in SPEC.md.
